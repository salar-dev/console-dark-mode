## Post 2 — reply to post 1 (how to install and use)

```
How to install:

1. Open the link above and click "Add to Chrome".
2. Open Play Console or App Store Connect. It is already dark.
3. Switch each console on or off from the toolbar popup, or press Alt+Shift+D.

No data is collected.

Open source (MIT): https://github.com/salar-dev/console-dark-mode
```
